The Firm

Elite athletic pedigree. Institutional rigor.

Ascent Sports Capital is a sports-focused investment firm investing across leagues, teams, and the infrastructure that supports them.

Elite athletic DNA

Founded by a Pro Lacrosse Hall of Famer, a WNBA first-round pick, and Division I athletes with deep relationships across professional leagues and the people who run them.

Institutional capital discipline

Rooted in more than $1B of transaction history, M&A law, and real estate advisory. We apply rigorous capital allocation to sports and the infrastructure around it.

The opportunity

High-growth sports assets, led by women's professional sports, where participation, visibility, and media value are rising on durable long-term fundamentals.

Our Edge

Built from the inside

We have spent our lives inside locker rooms, front offices, and league meetings. That operating experience helps us find value where traditional capital sees only complexity.

Our relationships with league governance and franchise ownership give us access to opportunities before they reach the broader market. Founders and leagues work with us because we understand their business from the field up.

Sports Community Impact

Capital that gives back to the game

2%of GP proceeds committed to the sports community

Where equity can't reach

Ascent's general partner commits 2% of its proceeds to nonprofits working on the hardest problems in sports, where access, safety, and opportunity for athletes depend on mission-driven organizations. As our returns grow, so does our contribution.

Already on the field

Ascent is the jersey sponsor of Real Colorado's USL League Two and USL W League teams, giving men's and women's amateur athletes a pre-professional platform to keep playing. Participation creates the pipeline, and the pipeline creates the value.
